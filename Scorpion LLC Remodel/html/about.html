Our Story & Values

Scorpion Construction is a locally operated, family-owned and Latino-owned construction business based in Fort Morgan, Colorado. With over 11 years of hands-on experience in the trade, we have built our reputation on quality craftsmanship, integrity, and reliable service.

We understand that undertaking a home improvement or structural project is a major investment. That’s why we offer free, in-person estimates and maintain full licensing and insurance to give our clients complete confidence in every job we undertake.

As a bilingual company speaking both English and Spanish, we take pride in serving every corner of our local community with open communication and honest advice.
